This is a place for half-formed ideas. Nothing here has to be finished, polished, or even right.

The rules are simple:

- Plant small. A note can be three lines. See [[how-to-tend-this-garden]].
- Mark honestly how grown each note is: seedling, budding, or evergreen.
- Link generously with `[[double brackets]]`. Links are how a pile of notes becomes a garden.

> The goal isn't a perfect archive. It's a habit of noticing.

Related: [[small-notes-win]].
